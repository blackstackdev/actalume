# Feature inventory

## Product identity

Actalume is the human-governed system of record for agent work. Its plain-language product description is Agent Work Ledger; its technical descriptor is the evidence layer for agentic work.

## Product loop

Contract → proposal → evidence → human decision → canonical history.

## Safety properties

- The agent cannot approve or reject.
- Review cannot be requested without evidence.
- Approval requires a named human and a second confirmation surface.
- Approved bricks cannot be edited or receive more evidence.
- A new brick cannot replace undecided work.
- Every transition is attributed in the visible activity ledger.

## Deliberate exclusions

- authentication and real customer records
- external storage and analytics
- social posting, messaging or deployment
- hidden automation or silent background actions
