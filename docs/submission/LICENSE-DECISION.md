# Open-source licence decision

The challenge requires a visible open-source licence at the top of the public repository.

## Approved: MIT

MIT is short, permissive and familiar to hackathon judges. It allows use, modification and distribution while preserving the copyright and permission notice and disclaiming warranty.

Brand-only attribution was approved on 2026-08-30. The standard MIT text is included at root `LICENSE` under `Copyright (c) 2026 Krēˈādiv Worx`.

## Alternative: Apache-2.0

Apache-2.0 is also permissive and includes an explicit patent licence, but it is longer and adds notice obligations. Nothing in the current project requires that additional machinery for challenge entry.

This document records the selected project licence and is not legal advice.
