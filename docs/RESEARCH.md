## Convergent winner patterns

1. **A real problem with a specific owner beats a broad platform pitch.** Winners start with a person under pressure, a workflow that breaks, or knowledge that disappears.
2. **The product makes one difficult truth visible.** Human confirmation, urgency versus capacity, deterministic acoustics, or promises versus code become spatial interactions rather than paragraphs.
3. **AI is bounded by deterministic systems.** The best entries state what the model may do and what it may not decide.
4. **The demo is one continuous transformation.** Input, reasoning, evidence, decision and result happen without detours.
5. **Proof is part of the interface and package.** Tests, live use, receipts, diagrams, source links and explicit limitations signal seriousness.
6. **Design serves the domain.** Warm editorial restraint fits accessibility and care; dense calibrated tooling fits spatial audio. None of the winners look premium by adding decoration alone.

## Ideas deliberately rejected

- A generic Kanban board: familiar, but it hides the agent/human boundary and loses novelty.
- A chat-first surface: it makes the transcript the product instead of the work record.
- Autonomous status changes: they weaken the central promise and duplicate existing agent demos.
- A landing-page-first build: judges need the real workflow immediately.
- Neon agent theatre, animated orbit diagrams and fake metrics: attractive for seconds, weak as operational proof.
- Importing a real private project archive: unnecessary privacy risk for a public challenge demo.

## Three principles to implement now

1. Make the five-stage custody line the product's visual and interaction spine.
2. Keep one dominant pending brick and one consequential decision in the first viewport.
3. Treat every claim as a receipt: actor, timestamp, evidence, state transition and approver.

## States to capture after implementation

- Ready state with a selected project contract.
- Agent-proposed brick stopped at the decision boundary.
- Expanded evidence receipts.
- Human edit/approve/reject state.
- Canonical history after approval.
- Empty project state.
- Invalid evidence and blocked self-approval state.
- Mobile review state.
